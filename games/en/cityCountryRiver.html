City, Country, River

One letter, many words · 2–12 players · Free scoring
Known as Scattergories

What is it?

The classic with paper and pen: find a city, a country, a river and more for one first letter.

How it’s played

You need: paper and pen for everyone; each writes the categories as columns, e.g. city, country, river, name, animal, job.

A round: the app draws a letter that has not come up in this match yet. Everyone writes one word for each category with this first letter. Whoever fills all columns first calls "Stop" and everyone stops; with the clock, the round ends when time is up.

Scoring: the answers are read out in turn. Your own answer scores 10 points, one that someone else has too scores 5. If only one person has any answer in a category, it scores 20. No answer scores nothing. The table decides together whether a word counts.

End: after as many rounds as you agreed, or whenever you like. The most points win.

In the app you tap "–", "double" or "own" per category and person. Categories, points, number of rounds, time and letters that are not drawn are set in the rules.

Goal

Score the most points with your own, preferably rare answers.

Scoring at a glance

The app draws a letter, everyone writes a word with it for each category. Own answer 10, double 5, the only answer in a category 20 points. The most points win.

Keeping score in the app

The app draws the letter and runs the clock if you like. Afterwards you only mark per category whether someone had an answer and whether it was a double – the app works out the points.
