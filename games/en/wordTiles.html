Word Tiles

Lay words, use the premium squares · 2–4 players · Board and tile games
Known as Scrabble

What is it?

The well-known game of laying words from letter tiles on a board with premium squares. You need the board, the tiles and the racks.

How it’s played

You need: the board with 15 × 15 squares, the letter tiles in their bag and a rack of seven tiles for everyone.

A turn: from your tiles, lay a word in a row or column that joins words already on the board; the first word covers the centre square. Every new word that forms counts, across as well. Then you draw back up to seven tiles. Instead of laying, you may pass or exchange tiles.

Scoring: every tile has a value. A letter square doubles or triples the tile on it, a word square the whole word; two word squares in one word multiply. Premiums count only for a tile newly laid on them. A blank scores 0. Laying all seven tiles in one turn adds 50.

End: once the bag is empty and someone lays their last tile, the game ends. Everyone subtracts the value of the tiles they still hold, and whoever went out first gains their sum.

In the app you type the word and tap the tiles on premium squares until 2×, 3× or blank shows; you choose the word premium below. Cross words come in with “Another word”. The letter set (German, English, French, Dutch, Italian, Spanish) and the end scoring are in the rules.

Goal

Score the most points with the words you lay.

Scoring at a glance

Per turn the word laid and every new cross word: the sum of the tile values, letter squares 2× or 3× on the tile, word squares 2× or 3× on the word, blanks 0. All seven tiles: +50. At the end everyone subtracts the tiles left, and whoever went out first gains them.

Keeping score in the app

You type the word laid and tap the letters on premium squares. The app scores letter values, double and triple squares, cross words, 50 points for all seven tiles and the tiles left at the end, with the values of six languages.
