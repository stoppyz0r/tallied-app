Mill

Three in a row, then take a piece · 2 players · Board and tile games
Known as Nine Men’s Morris

What is it?

An old board game on three nested squares with 24 points. Each side places and moves nine pieces and tries to form mills.

How it’s played

Placing: In turn each side places one of its nine pieces on an empty point.

Mill: Three of your pieces in one line form a mill. Whoever closes one removes an opposing piece, if possible not one from a mill.

Moving: Once all pieces are placed, take turns moving a piece along a line to an empty neighboring point. Opening and closing a mill again takes another piece.

Flying: A side with only three pieces left may jump to any empty point.

End: Whoever has only two pieces left or cannot move loses.

Goal

Take so many of the other side's pieces that it has only two left or cannot move.

Scoring at a glance

Three in a line make a mill and take an opposing piece. With three pieces you may fly; whoever has only two or cannot move loses.

Keeping score in the app

The board game for two with nine pieces each. After each game you tap the win or the draw – the app keeps the encounter and the ratings.
