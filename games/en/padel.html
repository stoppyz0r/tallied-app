Padel

Tennis in a glass cage, mostly in fours · 2, 4 players · Sports

What is it?

A racket game with short stringless paddles on a small court with glass walls that are in play; almost always doubles.

How it’s played

Serve: underarm; the ball bounces behind the baseline first and is struck at most at hip height, diagonally into the service box opposite. As in tennis you have two tries per point; the serve changes after every game, and all four serve in turn.

Walls: After bouncing in your own half, the ball may hit the glass wall and then be played back. But it must bounce in the other half before it touches a wall.

Points: You lose a point if you let the ball bounce twice, or hit it into the net, the fence or straight onto the other side’s wall.

Scoring: as in tennis 15, 30, 40, game. At deuce the next point usually decides; at tournaments the star point applies: advantage twice, and at the third deuce the next point decides.

Set and match: A set goes to 6 games with two games ahead, at 6:6 a tiebreak to 7 follows. Matches go to two sets, often with a match tiebreak to 10 instead of the third. The players change ends after the odd games.

Goal

Be the first side to win the agreed number of sets; scoring is as in tennis.

Scoring at a glance

Scored as in tennis: 15, 30, 40, game, sets to 6, tiebreak at 6:6. At deuce the next point usually decides. Almost always in fours.

Keeping score in the app

Doubles on a walled court. After each point you tap who won it – the app counts as in tennis, with the deciding point at deuce, the tiebreak and the match tiebreak, says who serves and when to change ends, and shows break, set and match points.
