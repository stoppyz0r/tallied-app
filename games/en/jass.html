Jass (Schieber)

Four players in teams, with declarations, Stöck and factors · 4 players · Card games

What is it?

Switzerland's best-known card game with 36 cards. In Schieber, the two players sitting opposite each other play together; whoever names trumps may also push the choice to their partner.

How it’s played

Cards: 36 cards, in each suit ace, king, over, under, banner (ten), nine to six. Each player gets nine cards. The player in first position names trumps or pushes the choice to their partner.

Trumps: In the trump suit the under (jack) is the highest card (20 points), then the nell (trump nine, 14). Obenabe means no trumps, ace high; Undenufe no trumps, six high.

Tricks: You must follow suit but may always trump; you never have to follow with the trump under. Whoever takes the trick leads.

Counting: The cards hold 152 points, the last trick brings 5 more, 157 in all. One team counts its tricks, the other has the rest. A team taking every trick makes a match: 257.

Declarations and Stöck: Before the first trick you declare sequences (three 20, four 50, five 100 …) and four of a kind (four unders 200, four nines 150, otherwise 100). Only the team with the highest declaration scores. King and over of trumps together are the Stöck, 20 points.

Factor: Depending on the trump, all points count once, twice or three times. Play to the target.

Goal

Be the first team to reach the target, usually 2500 with factors or 1000 without.

Scoring at a glance

157 points per game, a match 257; only the team with the highest declaration scores it, Stöck 20. Everything counts times the trump's factor.

Keeping score in the app

The Swiss card game for four in two teams. For each game you tap the trump, the counted card points, a match, declarations and the Stöck – the app applies the trump's factor and keeps score to the target.
