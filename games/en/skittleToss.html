Finnish Skittles

Knock down twelve pins, hit exactly 50 · 2–12 players · Ball, throwing and lane games

What is it?

A throwing game from Finland: from a few meters you throw a wooden stick at twelve numbered wooden pins standing close together. Fallen pins are stood up again where they landed, so they spread out as the game goes on.

How it’s played

Setup: The twelve pins stand close together in four rows, from the front: 1 and 2; 3, 10 and 4; 5, 11, 12 and 6; 7, 9 and 8. The throwing line is about 3 to 4 meters in front of them. Draw lots for who starts; after that everyone always throws in the same order.

Throwing: Each player throws the stick once, underhand, at the pins. A pin counts as down when it lies flat; one leaning on another pin or on the stick does not count.

Points: If exactly one pin falls, its number counts. If several fall, their count does – three pins give 3 points, whichever they are. Then every fallen pin is stood up exactly where it lies.

Exactly 50: Whoever reaches exactly 50 points wins at once. Whoever goes over falls back to 25 and plays on.

Misses: If no pin falls, it is a miss. Whoever misses three times in a row is out. If only one player is left, they have won.

Goal

Be the first to reach exactly 50 points.

Scoring at a glance

One fallen pin counts its number, several their count. Whoever reaches exactly 50 wins; going over means back to 25. Three misses in a row and you are out.

Keeping score in the app

Outdoor throwing game with twelve numbered wooden pins. After each throw you tap the pins that fell – the app works out the points, sets anyone over 50 back to 25, counts the misses and knows the winner.
