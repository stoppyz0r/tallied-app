French Tarot

78 cards, one taker against the rest · 3–5 players · Card games

What is it?

The French card game with 78 cards: four suits with knights, 21 trumps and the excuse. One player takes and plays against the others, at five with a called partner.

How it’s played

Cards: 21 trumps, the excuse and four suits of 14 cards (king, queen, knight, jack, 10 to 1). The three oudlers are the 1 (petit), the 21 and the excuse.

Bidding: The taker bids prise, garde, garde sans or garde contre; the higher, the more the deal counts. With prise and garde the taker picks up the dog and discards as many cards.

Points: Kings and oudlers count 4½, queens 3½, knights 2½, jacks 1½, all others ½; 91 in all. The taker needs 56 points without an oudler, 51 with one, 41 with two, 36 with three.

Bonuses: petit au bout when the petit falls in the last trick; a handful for showing many trumps; a slam for all tricks.

At five the taker calls a king; whoever holds it plays with them.

Goal

Collect the most points over the deals; as taker, make enough card points for your oudlers.

Scoring at a glance

The taker needs 56, 51, 41 or 36 points with 0 to 3 oudlers. The deal is worth 25 plus the margin, times 1, 2, 4 or 6 by contract, plus petit au bout, handfuls and slam. Each defender pays or receives it, the taker the multiple.

Keeping score in the app

For each deal you enter the taker, the contract, the oudlers and the points, plus petit au bout, handfuls and slam. The app scores after the official French rules, the half point included, and splits the score between taker, partner and defence.
