Mia

Roll in secret, call higher, bluff · 2–10 players · Dice games

What is it?

Mia, known in German as Mäxchen or Meiern, is a bluffing game with two dice, a cup and a lid or coaster. The higher die counts as the tens, doubles beat all other numbers, and the highest is 21, the Mia. Whoever fibs and gets caught loses a life.

How it’s played

Setup: Two dice lie under a cup on a lid. Everyone starts with the same number of lives, usually six. Draw lots for who starts; play goes clockwise.

Ranking: The higher die is the tens and the lower die the ones – a 5 and a 3 make 53. Normal numbers rise from 31 (the lowest) to 65. Above them are the doubles from 11 to 66, and at the very top is 21, the Mia.

Calling: On your turn, roll in secret, look under the cup alone and call a value. It must be higher than the previous call and may be true or a bluff. Then pass the closed cup to the next player.

Believe or lift: Whoever gets the cup decides: if you believe the call, roll again in secret and call an even higher value, bluffing if need be. If you doubt it, lift the cup. If the dice show less than called, the caller loses; if they show at least as much, the one who lifted the cup loses. That ends the round.

Mia: Nothing beats a Mia. If a Mia is called to you, lift the cup or give the round up. In a round that was about a Mia, the loser loses two lives instead of one.

End of the game: The loser starts the next round; if they are out, the next player starts. Anyone with no lives left is out. The last player left wins.

Goal

See through bluffs, bluff convincingly yourself and keep your lives. The last player with lives left wins.

Scoring at a glance

The higher die is the tens; doubles beat normal numbers, and the Mia (21) beats everything. On a reveal, the caller loses if the dice show less, otherwise the one who lifted the cup. A lost round costs a life, two with a Mia. The loser starts the next round; the last player with lives wins.

Keeping score in the app

Bluffing dice game with two dice under a cup: players take turns rolling in secret and calling an ever higher value, true or bluffed; the next player believes it or lifts the cup. Whoever loses a round loses a life, two with Mia. You tap who lost – the app keeps track of lives and who is out.
