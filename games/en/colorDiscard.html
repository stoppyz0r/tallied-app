Discard!

Color on color, number on number · 2–10 players · Card games
Known as UNO

What is it?

A shedding game with number cards in four colors – red, yellow, green and blue. Skip, reverse, draw 2 and wild cards with or without draw 4 shake up the game. Anyone down to one card has to call it out.

How it’s played

Equipment and setup: The deck has 108 cards: in each color one zero, two of each number from 1 to 9 and two each of skip, reverse and draw 2; plus four wild and four wild draw 4 cards. Everyone gets seven cards, and the rest is placed face down as the draw pile. Turn over the top card to start the discard pile. The player to the dealer’s left starts, and play moves clockwise.

Taking a turn: On your turn, play one card that matches the top card’s color, number or symbol. A wild card lets you choose the next color. If you can’t or don’t want to play, draw one card; you may play only that card, and only right away if it fits. Otherwise the next player is up. When the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discards except for the top card to make a new one.

Action cards:

  • Skip: The next player misses their turn.
  • Reverse: The direction of play changes; with two players it works like a skip.
  • Draw 2: The next player draws two cards and misses their turn. The penalty can’t be passed on with another draw card.
  • Wild draw 4: The next player draws four cards and misses their turn. You may only play it if you have no card of the current color. Anyone who doubts this can check your hand: if the play was illegal, you draw four cards; otherwise the challenger draws six instead of four.

Starting card: If the first card turned over is a skip, the first player misses their turn; with a draw 2, they draw two cards and miss their turn. With a reverse, the dealer starts and play goes counterclockwise. With a wild, the first player chooses the color. A wild draw 4 is shuffled back in and replaced by a new card.

End of the round: When you play your next-to-last card, call out that you have one card left. If a missed call is noticed before the next player plays, you draw two cards. The round ends as soon as someone plays their last card; a draw card played last still takes effect.

Scoring: Number cards count their face value, skip, reverse and draw 2 count 20 points each, and both kinds of wild count 50 points each. Depending on the chosen scoring, the round’s winner collects everyone else’s remaining points, or everyone writes down their own remaining points as penalties.

End of the game: The game ends as soon as someone reaches the agreed limit after a round, usually 500 points. When collecting points, the highest total wins; with penalty points, the lowest.

Goal

Be the first to get rid of all your cards. Once someone reaches the point limit (usually 500), the highest or lowest total wins, depending on the scoring.

Scoring at a glance

The first empty hand wins the round. Cards left in hand count: number cards their face value; skip, reverse and draw 2 count 20 each; wild and wild draw 4 count 50 each. Depending on the scoring, the round’s winner collects everyone else’s remaining points, or everyone collects their own as penalties. The game ends as soon as someone reaches the limit after a round.

Keeping score in the app

Shedding game with four colors, numbers from 0 to 9 and action cards: play color on color or number on number. Whoever empties their hand first wins the round. You enter the cards left in the others’ hands, and the app counts the points using the chosen scoring.
