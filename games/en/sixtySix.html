Sixty-Six

Two players racing to 66, game to 7 · 2 players · Card games

What is it?

A trick-taking game for two with 24 cards (20 in Austria) and trumps. Whoever first has 66 card points in their tricks wins the game; king and queen of a suit bring 20 or 40 as a pair.

How it’s played

Cards: ace 11, ten 10, king 4, queen 3, jack 2 (nines 0 if used). Each player gets six cards (five in Schnapsen); the next card is placed face up under the stock as trumps.

Tricks: The leader plays a card and the other adds one. The higher card of the led suit or a trump takes the trick. Then both draw from the stock, the trick winner first. While the stock lasts you need neither follow suit nor trump.

Marriages: king and queen of one suit together bring 20 card points, in trumps 40; only the leader may call them. The trump jack may be swapped for the face-up trump card.

Closing: Whoever thinks they can reach 66 with the cards in hand turns the trump card over. From then on, and once the stock is empty: follow suit and trump if you can.

Points: Whoever reaches 66 calls it and scores 1 point; if the opponent has fewer than 33 card points, 2 (schneider); with no trick, 3 (schwarz). Whoever closes or calls 66 and fails gives the opponent 2 points, or 3 if the opponent had no trick when it was closed.

Round to 7: The first to 7 points wins.

Goal

Win games and be the first to collect 7 points.

Scoring at a glance

First to 66 card points wins the game: 1 point, 2 if the opponent has under 33, 3 if the opponent has no trick. A failed closing gives the opponent 2 or 3. Play to 7 points.

Keeping score in the app

The trick-taking game for two, known in Austria as Schnapsen. After each game you tap who won and how – the app gives 1, 2 or 3 points and spots the winner of the round to 7.
