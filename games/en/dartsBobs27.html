Darts Bob's 27

Doubles practice from D1 to bull · 1–8 players · Darts

What is it?

A practice game named after the professional who invented it: round by round you throw at the doubles from 1 to 20 and finally at the bull.

How it’s played

Start: Everyone begins with 27 points.

Rounds: In round 1 you aim at double 1, in round 2 at double 2 and so on up to double 20, and finally at the double bull. Each round is three darts.

Points: Every hit in the round's double adds its value, so 14 per hit on double 7. If none of the three darts hits, the double's value comes off once.

Out: Whoever drops to 0 or below is out.

End: After the bull the highest score wins. As practice, your own record counts.

Goal

Get through with as many points as possible without dropping to 0.

Scoring at a glance

Start at 27, rounds D1 to D20 and double bull. Hits add the double, a visit without one takes it off; at 0 you are out.

Keeping score in the app

The well-known doubles practice, alone or against each other. You tap each dart – the app adds hits, takes off without one and says who is out.
