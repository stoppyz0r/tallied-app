Phase Rummy

Complete ten phases in order · 2–6 players · Card games

What is it?

A rummy game with number cards from 1 to 12 in four colors, jokers and skip cards. Everyone works through ten set phases, such as two sets of three, a run of eight or seven cards of one color. Only those who lay down their phase move on to the next one in the following round.

How it’s played

Setup: The deck has 108 cards: the numbers 1 to 12 twice in each of four colors, eight jokers and four skip cards. Everyone gets ten cards and starts with phase 1. The rest is placed face down as the draw pile, and one card is turned face up to start the discard pile. The player to the dealer’s left starts, and play moves clockwise.

The phases: A set is cards with the same number (set of three, set of four …), a run is consecutive numbers in any colors.
1. Two sets of three
2. One set of three and one run of four
3. One set of four and one run of four
4. One run of seven
5. One run of eight
6. One run of nine
7. Two sets of four
8. Seven cards of one color
9. One set of five and one set of two
10. One set of five and one set of three

Taking a turn: On your turn, draw the top face-down card or the top card of the discard pile. You may only lay down your phase completely and all at once. Jokers stand in for any number and any color, but every phase needs at least one real number card. Once you have laid down your phase, from your next turn on you may add matching cards to any phase on the table. Finish your turn by discarding one card.

Skip cards: When you discard a skip card, choose another player who has to miss their next turn. Skip cards can’t be taken from the discard pile or used in a phase. If the first card turned up is a skip card, the first player is skipped. When the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discard pile except for the top card to make a new one.

End of the round: Whoever gets rid of all their cards ends the round. Everyone who laid down their phase this round plays the next phase in the following round; everyone else tries their phase again. You can move up at most one phase per round.

Scoring: Leftover cards from 1 to 9 count 5 points each, from 10 to 12 count 10, skip cards 15 and jokers 25.

End of the game: Once someone completes the tenth phase in a round, the game ends after that round. Whoever completed the most phases is ahead; with equal phases, the fewest penalty points decide.

Goal

Complete all ten phases. The game ends after the round in which someone completes the tenth phase; then phases decide first, and then the fewest penalty points.

Scoring at a glance

Whoever lays down their whole phase plays the next phase in the following round. Whoever runs out of cards first ends the round. Leftover cards: 1 to 9 count 5 points each, 10 to 12 count 10, skip 15, joker 25. After the round in which someone completes the tenth phase, the most phases win, and on a tie the fewest points.

Keeping score in the app

Rummy variant with ten fixed tasks, the phases, such as two sets of three or a run of eight. Whoever completes their phase moves up; leftover cards count as penalties. You enter who completed their phase and the leftover cards, and the app tracks phases and points.
