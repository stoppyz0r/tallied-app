Table Tennis

To 11, two points ahead · 2, 4 players · Sports

What is it?

The racket game at a table with a net, paddles and a small ball, played as singles or doubles.

How it’s played

Serve: The ball rests on the open palm, is tossed up at least 16 cm and struck so that it bounces first on your own side, then on the other. If it touches the net and still bounces correctly, the serve is replayed. In doubles the serve goes diagonally from the right half to the right half.

Change: The serve changes after every two points, from 10:10 after every point. In the next set the other side starts. In doubles the partners hit in turn.

Points: A point goes to whoever last returned the ball correctly – when the other side fails to return it after one bounce, hits it out or into the net, or touches it twice.

Set and match: A set goes to 11; at 10:10 whoever is two points ahead first wins. Matches are usually played to three sets.

Goal

Be the first to win the agreed number of sets; a set goes to 11 with two points ahead.

Scoring at a glance

A set goes to 11, from 10:10 with two points ahead. The serve changes every two points, from 10:10 after every point. Usually to three sets.

Keeping score in the app

Singles or doubles at the table. After each set you enter the score – the app checks that it is a valid set, counts the sets won and says who serves in the next set. If you like, count point by point, and it shows the serve, the change of ends and set points too.
