Snooker

Red, colour, red – breaks up to 147 · 2 players · Sports

What is it?

The billiards game on the large table with 15 reds and six colours, played by two over several frames. You need the table, the 22 balls and two cues.

How it’s played

Break-off: The breaker plays from the “D” into the racked reds. After that the players take turns; whoever pots stays at the table, and the points of one visit are the break.

Balls: First a red (1), then a colour of your choice: yellow 2, green 3, brown 4, blue 5, pink 6, black 7. Colours are re-spotted while reds remain. Once the reds are gone, the colours are potted in order from yellow to black.

Fouls: Hitting or potting the wrong ball, potting the cue ball or missing everything gives the opponent at least 4 points, at most 7. After a foul, an opponent who cannot see a ball on directly may play another ball as a free ball.

Frame and match: A frame ends with the last black or when someone concedes; on a tie the black is re-spotted. Matches are usually the best of an odd number of frames.

Goal

Win more frames than your opponent; a frame goes to whoever scores more points in it.

Scoring at a glance

Red 1, then a colour: yellow 2, green 3, brown 4, blue 5, pink 6, black 7. With no reds left, the colours in order. A foul gives the opponent 4 to 7. Whoever scores more wins the frame.

Keeping score in the app

You tap the balls potted in a visit and enter fouls. The app only allows the ball on, scores breaks and fouls, counts the reds on the table, the points remaining and snookers needed, keeps the frames and praises centuries.
