Darts Checkout 121

Nine darts for 121, then higher · 1–8 players · Darts

What is it?

A popular darts practice for finishing: with three visits you bring a number from 121 up to exactly zero, the last dart a double – like the end of a leg of 501.

How it’s played

Setup: You need a dartboard and three darts each. Beforehand, agree on what happens after a missed target and whether the game ends after a number of targets or of visits.

Target and rest: Everyone starts with the target 121. As in X01, the points of each visit come off what’s left, which starts at the target.

Checking out: You have three visits per target, so nine darts. The last dart must hit a double that brings what’s left to exactly zero – for example double 16 with 32 left. Make it and you move on to the next higher target.

Bust: If what’s left drops below zero, leaves exactly 1, or reaches zero without a double, the visit scores nothing and you go back to what you had before it.

Missed: If anything is left after the third visit, the target is missed. Usually you go one lower, but never below 121; if you like, the target stays or goes back to 121.

Taking turns: With several players, each throws one visit, then the next; everyone has a target of their own.

End of the game: The game ends after the agreed number of targets (usually ten) or visits. Ahead is whoever checked out the highest target, then whoever made more targets, then whoever needed fewer darts for them.

Goal

Check out as high a target as you can and make as many targets as possible.

Scoring at a glance

From 121, nine darts per target to check out on a double. Make it and go one higher, miss it and go one lower.

Keeping score in the app

Checkout practice: check out 121 in nine darts or fewer, and the target moves up by one. Alone or taking turns. You enter the total or tap dart by dart, and the app works out what’s left, spots busts and checkouts, suggests checkout routes and keeps track of the target and your best.
