Darts Halve-It

Hit the target – or be halved · 1–8 players · Darts

What is it?

A pub and practice game: each round has a different target, sometimes a number, sometimes any double or triple, the bull last.

How it’s played

Play: Everyone starts with 40 points and throws three darts in turn. The rounds' targets are 15, 16, any double, 17, 18, any triple, 19, 20 and the bull.

Points: Every dart in the target counts its value, so triple 15 is 45, double 20 on “double” 40, the bull 25 or 50.

Halving: If none of the three darts hits the target, the score is halved, rounded down.

End: After the bull round the highest score wins.

Goal

Have the highest score after nine rounds.

Scoring at a glance

Targets 15, 16, double, 17, 18, triple, 19, 20, bull. Hits count their value; without a hit the score is halved.

Keeping score in the app

Darts game with nine targets in a row. Hits count, a visit without any hit halves the score. You tap each dart, and the app knows each round's target and does the maths.
