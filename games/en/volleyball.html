Volleyball

Two teams, sets to 25 · 2–12 players · Sports

What is it?

The team game over a high net, indoors or on sand as beach volleyball.

How it’s played

Serve: From behind the end line the ball is hit over the net into the other court. Whoever wins the rally serves next; indoors the team moves on by one position.

Points: Each team may touch the ball at most three times before it must go over the net, nobody twice in a row. A team scores when the ball touches the floor in the other court or the other team makes a fault – out, net, four touches.

Set and match: Indoors a set goes to 25, the fifth to 15, each with two points ahead; the first team to three sets wins. In beach volleyball, usually two against two, a set goes to 21, the third to 15, with two sets to win.

Change of ends: indoors after every set and at 8 points in the deciding set, on the beach every 7 points, every 5 in the third set.

Goal

Be the first team to win three sets (beach: two); a set goes to 25 (beach: 21), the deciding set to 15, always with two points ahead.

Scoring at a glance

Every rally scores a point. Indoor: sets to 25, the fifth to 15, three sets to win. Beach: sets to 21, the third to 15, two sets to win. Always two points ahead.

Keeping score in the app

Two teams of any size, indoors or on the beach. Enter the score after each set, or count point by point – then the app says who serves and when to change ends, and shows set and match points.
