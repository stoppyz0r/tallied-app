Tute

Twenties, forties and the tute · 2–4 players · Card games

What is it?

Spain’s classic trick-taking game with 40 cards: holding king and horse of a suit lets you declare 20, or 40 in trumps (“las cuarenta”). For two, three, or four in two pairs.

How it’s played

Cards: 40 Spanish cards (oros, copas, espadas, bastos). Rank and values: ace 11, three 10, king 4, horse 3, jack 2, then 7, 6, 5, 4, 2 with no points – 120 in all.

Playing: All cards are dealt, the last one shows trumps (triunfo). You must follow suit, beat the trick if you can and otherwise trump. Whoever wins a trick may declare (cantar): king and horse of a suit count 20, in trumps 40. Declaring four kings or four horses is a tute and wins the game at once.

Points: the card points of the tricks won, 10 for the last trick (“diez de últimas”) and the declarations. Most points win the game; on a tie, whoever took the last trick.

Goal

Be the first side to win the agreed number of games (4 by default).

Scoring at a glance

Card points (ace 11, three 10, king 4, horse 3, jack 2) plus 10 for the last trick and the declarations (20, in trumps 40). Most points win the game, a tie goes to the last trick; a tute wins it at once. First to the agreed number of games wins.

Keeping score in the app

For each hand you enter the card points – with the card calculator or as a number –, who took the last trick and the declarations. The app adds the points, decides the game and counts the games to the win.
