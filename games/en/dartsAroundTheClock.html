Darts Around the Clock

Hit 1 to 20 in order · 1–8 players · Darts

What is it?

A race around the dartboard that also works as solo practice. Everyone works their way from 1 to 20, in number order rather than around the board, and finishes with the bull. Every hit on the number you currently need moves you one step on.

How it’s played

Setup: You need a dartboard, three darts each and a throwing line. Everyone starts on 1, and players take turns throwing a visit of up to three darts.

Hitting: Only a hit on your own next number counts; single, double and triple are equally good. Other numbers and misses don’t count, and you can’t skip ahead. After a hit, the next number is up straight away, even in the same visit: if your first dart hits the 1, your second goes at the 2.

Bull: After the 20 you still need to hit the bull; the outer bull and the bullseye both count.

End of the game: Whoever hits the last target first is through; their remaining darts are dropped. The current round is still finished so that everyone has the same number of turns; anyone who also gets through in it shares the win. After that, whoever got furthest is ahead, and on a tie whoever needed fewer darts.

Variants: Without the bull, the game ends at 20. It gets harder if only doubles or only triples count; with doubles only, just the bullseye counts at the end. It goes faster if a double moves you two steps and a triple three. You can also stop as soon as someone gets through instead of finishing the round.

Goal

Be the first to hit all numbers from 1 to 20 and the bull in order.

Scoring at a glance

Everyone takes turns throwing up to three darts at their next number, from 1 to 20, then the bull. Each hit on that number moves you one step on, whether single, double or triple. Whoever gets through first wins; the round is finished, and anyone who also gets through in it shares the win.

Keeping score in the app

Darts game in which everyone hits the numbers 1 to 20 in order, finishing with the bull. Only your own next number counts; whoever gets through first wins. You tap each dart, and the app shows the next target and spots the winner.
