Mau-Mau

Match suit or rank, jacks pick a suit · 2–6 players · Card games
Known as Crazy Eights

What is it?

The traditional shedding game with cards from 7 to ace. A 7 makes the next player draw two cards, an 8 skips them, and a jack lets you call a suit. You announce your last card with “Mau” and play it with “Mau-Mau”.

How it’s played

Setup: Shuffle 32 cards from 7 to ace and deal five cards to everyone. The rest is placed face down as the draw pile, and one card is turned up next to it. The player to the dealer’s left starts, and play moves clockwise.

Taking a turn: On your turn, play a card of the same suit or rank as the top card. You may play a jack on any card. If you can’t play, draw one card; if it fits, you may play it right away, otherwise the next player is up. When the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discard pile except for the top card to make a new one.

Special cards:

  • 7: The next player draws two cards and misses their turn.
  • 8: The next player misses their turn.
  • Jack: Whoever plays it calls a suit; the next card must be of that suit or another jack.

End of the round: Whoever has only one card left says “Mau”. Whoever plays their last card says “Mau-Mau” and ends the round.

Scoring: The player who went out gets 0 points. Everyone else writes down their leftover cards as penalty points: ace 11, king 4, queen 3, jack 20, 7 to 10 at face value.

End of the game: The game ends as soon as someone reaches the agreed limit after a round, usually 100 points. The lowest total wins.

Goal

Be the first to get rid of all your cards and collect few penalty points. Once someone reaches the limit, the lowest total wins.

Scoring at a glance

Whoever plays all their cards first goes out and scores 0. Everyone else scores their leftover cards as penalties: ace 11, king 4, queen 3, jack 20, 7 to 10 at face value. The game ends as soon as someone reaches the limit after a round; the lowest total wins.

Keeping score in the app

Shedding game with 32 cards: play suit on suit or rank on rank, while 7, 8 and jack shake things up. Whoever gets rid of all their cards first goes out, and the others write down the points in their hand. You tap who went out and enter the leftover cards – the app counts up to the limit.
