Scopa

Sweeps, settebello and primiera · 2, 3, 4, 6 players · Card games

What is it?

The Italian fishing game with 40 cards: you capture table cards that match your own, ideally clearing the whole table. For two, three or in teams, as scopone for four.

How it’s played

Cards: 40 Italian cards in four suits (coins, cups, swords, clubs), each ace to 7 and three face cards. With a French deck you use ace to 7 and the face cards, diamonds being the coins.

Playing: Everyone gets three cards, four lie face up. On your turn you play a card and capture an equal one from the table or several that add up to its value (face cards count 8, 9, 10). Clearing the table is a sweep (scopa).

Points: each sweep 1, most cards 1, most coins 1, the settebello (7 of coins) 1 and the primiera 1. For the primiera the best card per suit counts: 7 = 21, 6 = 18, ace = 16, 5 = 15, 4 = 14, 3 = 13, 2 = 12, face card = 10.

Goal

Be the first to reach 11 points, 21 in teams.

Scoring at a glance

Each sweep 1, most cards 1, most coins 1, settebello 1, primiera 1; on a tie nobody gets the point. First to 11 (21 in teams) wins.

Keeping score in the app

For each hand you enter each side's sweeps, how many cards and coins it took, who has the settebello and the best card per suit. The app scores cards, coins and the primiera and knows the winner at the target.
