Ten Thousand

Roll, set aside, push your luck · 1–10 players · Dice games
Known as Farkle

What is it?

The well-known game with six dice, also played as 5,000. All you need are dice and something to write with.

How it’s played

You need: six dice, everyone takes turns.

A turn: roll all six dice. Set aside at least one scoring die: a 1 scores 100, a 5 scores 50, three of a kind score the face times 100, three ones 1,000. Four, five or six of a kind double the value with each die. A straight 1 to 6 scores 1,500. Then decide: stop and write down the points of the turn, or roll on with the dice left. Once all six are set aside, you may roll all six again.

Bust: if a throw shows no scoring die, all points of that turn are lost and the next player rolls.

Entry: whoever has no points yet needs at least 350 in one turn, otherwise they roll on or bust.

End: the first to reach 10,000 wins. Only dice of one throw count together: a 5 from the first throw and two 5s from the second are not three of a kind.

In the app you tap the dice set aside per throw or enter only the sum; target, entry, straight, three pairs and a final round are house rules.

Goal

Be the first to reach 10,000 points.

Scoring at a glance

After each throw set aside scoring dice (1 = 100, 5 = 50, three of a kind = face × 100, three ones 1,000), then write down or roll on. A throw without a scoring die is a bust. To enter, at least 350 in one turn. The first to 10,000 wins.

Keeping score in the app

After each throw you set aside scoring dice and decide whether to roll on. The app scores ones, fives, three of a kind and straights, minds the entry minimum and counts to 10,000.
