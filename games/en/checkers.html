Checkers

Move diagonally, jump, crown a king · 2 players · Board and tile games

What is it?

A board game with twelve pieces each on the dark squares of a chessboard. Pieces move diagonally and capture by jumping over opposing ones.

How it’s played

Setup: Each side places twelve pieces on the dark squares of the three rows in front of it. Dark starts.

Moving: A piece moves one square diagonally forward onto an empty square.

Capturing: If an opposing piece stands diagonally in front with an empty square behind it, jump over it and remove it. If another jump is possible, keep jumping. Capturing is mandatory.

King: A piece reaching the far row becomes a king (a second piece on top). A king also moves and captures backward; in many rules it moves any distance.

End: Whoever has no pieces left or cannot move loses. If neither side can win any more, it is a draw.

Goal

Capture all opposing pieces or block them so the other side cannot move.

Scoring at a glance

Move diagonally, jump diagonally to capture, capturing is mandatory. Whoever cannot move loses.

Keeping score in the app

The board game for two on the dark squares. After each game you tap the win or the draw – the app keeps the encounter and the ratings.
