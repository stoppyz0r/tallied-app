Klaverjas

Four players, roem, nat and pit · 4 players · Card games

What is it?

The card game of the Netherlands: four players in two teams with 32 cards; one team chooses trumps and must score more than the other. Roem for runs and sets in a trick keeps it exciting.

How it’s played

Cards: 32 cards from 7 to ace. In trumps the jack counts 20, the 9 14, the ace 11, the 10 10, the king 4, the queen 3. In the other suits ace 11, 10 10, king 4, queen 3, jack 2. The last trick brings 10; 162 in all.

Playing: In turn each player plays or passes; whoever plays chooses trumps. If all pass, the dealer must play. In Amsterdam you need not overtrump when your partner wins the trick, in Rotterdam you always must; the scoring is the same.

Roem: Whoever wins a trick claims the roem in it: three cards of a suit in sequence 20, four in sequence 50, four of a kind 100, king and queen of trumps (“stuk”) 20.

Nat: The playing team must score more than the opponents, roem included, otherwise it goes nat and the opponents score 162 and all roem. Pit: If the playing team takes every trick, it gets 100 more. Revoke: Failing to follow suit or to overtrump when you could gives the opponents 262 and all roem.

Goal

Have the most points as a team after 16 deals.

Scoring at a glance

162 card points per deal, plus roem: three in sequence 20, four in sequence 50, four of a kind 100, stuk 20, pit 100. The playing team must score strictly more, otherwise it goes nat and the opponents score everything. 16 deals.

Keeping score in the app

For each deal you enter which team plays, the card points of one team, pit, roem, stuk and whether anyone revoked. The app decides whether the playing team holds or goes nat and ends the game after 16 deals.
