Boules

Get close to the jack, first to 13 · 2–6 players · Ball, throwing and lane games
Known as Pétanque

What is it?

Boules, or more precisely pétanque, is played with metal balls and a small wooden jack on gravel or firm sand. From a throwing circle you try to place your balls as close to the jack as possible or knock the opponents’ balls away. It is played one, two or three a side.

How it’s played

Setup: Two teams play against each other. Playing alone or in pairs, each player has three balls; in teams of three, two each. You also need a jack and a throwing circle on suitable ground. Draw lots for which team starts.

Starting a round: The starting team draws the throwing circle and throws the jack 6 to 10 meters. Then it throws the first ball as close to the jack as possible. While throwing, both feet stay inside the circle and on the ground until the ball has landed.

Order of play: Then the other team throws. From then on, the team whose best ball is farther from the jack always throws – until it is closer or has no balls left. When a team runs out of balls, the other throws its remaining ones. Team members may throw in any order.

Pointing and shooting: You can place a ball close to the jack or throw at another ball to knock it away. The jack may move too; what counts is where it ends up. Close distances are measured before anyone picks up balls.

Scoring a round: Once all balls are thrown, only the team with the closest ball scores: one point for each of its balls closer than the other team’s best. That makes at most three points playing alone and at most six in pairs and triples.

End of the game: The team that scored starts the next round where the jack was. Play until a team reaches 13 points. Winning 13 to 0 is a “Fanny”.

Goal

Get your balls closer to the jack than the other team. The first team to 13 points wins.

Scoring at a glance

After the first ball, the team that is farther away always throws until it is closer or out of balls. Once all balls are thrown, only the team with the closest ball scores, one point per ball closer than the other team’s best. Three balls each playing alone or in pairs, two in triples. The first team to 13 points wins.

Keeping score in the app

Ball game for two teams: one against one, two against two or three against three. Whoever is closer to the jack at the end of a round scores. You tap which team has how many balls closer – the app counts to 13, knows how many balls a team can score at most and knows the winner.
