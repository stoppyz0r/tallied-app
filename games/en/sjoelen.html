Dutch Shuffleboard

30 discs, four gates, sets count 20 · 1–12 players · Ball, throwing and lane games

What is it?

A skill game from the Netherlands: on a long wooden board you slide 30 wooden discs into four gates at the far end. Played at home, in clubs and in competitions.

How it’s played

A turn: You slide all 30 discs. Whatever lies in a gate stays there; you take the other discs back and slide them a second and a third time.

Gates: From left to right they count 2, 3, 4 and 1. A disc only counts when it lies fully in the gate.

Points: One disc in each of the four gates is a set and counts 20, twice the gate values together. Every other disc counts its gate's value. The most is 148 points.

Bonus: Reaching 148 after the first throw earns two slides of a bonus disc, after the second throw one; it counts its gate.

Goal

Have the most points after ten turns.

Scoring at a glance

20 per set (one disc in each gate), every other disc its gate's value 2, 3, 4 or 1. At most 148, with the bonus up to 156. Ten turns, most points win.

Keeping score in the app

The Dutch sliding game on the sjoelbak. After each turn you enter how many discs lie in gates 2, 3, 4 and 1 – the app counts sets and the rest, spots 148 with its bonus and knows the winner after ten turns.
