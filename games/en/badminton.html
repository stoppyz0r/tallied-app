Badminton

Shuttlecock over the net, sets to 21 · 2, 4 players · Sports

What is it?

The racket game with a shuttlecock over a high net, played as singles or doubles.

How it’s played

Serve: Hit from below the waist, diagonally into the service court opposite. With an even score the serving side serves from the right, with an odd score from the left.

Points: Every rally brings a point – to the side whose opponent fails to return the shuttle correctly: it lands in their own court, out or in the net. Whoever wins the point serves next.

Set and match: A set goes to 21; from 20:20 the first to lead by two points wins, at 29:29 the next point decides. Whoever wins two sets wins the match; the winner of a set serves first in the next.

Change of ends: after every set and in the third set as soon as a side has 11 points.

Goal

Be the first to win two sets; a set goes to 21 with two points ahead, at most to 30.

Scoring at a glance

Every rally scores a point. A set goes to 21, from 20:20 with two points ahead, at most to 30. Two sets to win.

Keeping score in the app

Singles or doubles in the hall or the garden. Enter the score after each set, or count point by point – then the app says who serves from which side and when to change ends, and shows set and match points.
