Word Guessing

Explain, draw, act – guess as a team · 4–16 players · Free scoring

What is it?

A team game in the style of the well-known explaining, drawing and acting games: one person describes words, their team guesses against the clock.

How it’s played

You need: words on cards or slips, e.g. from a game or written yourselves; paper for drawing.

Teams: form two to four teams of at least two. Teams take turns; within a team, the next person explains each time.

A turn: whoever explains starts the sand timer in the app and describes one word after the other, without saying the word itself, parts of it or translations. Their team guesses aloud. Tap "Guessed" for each word guessed and "Skipped" for each word skipped. When time runs out, the word in progress no longer counts.

Scoring: every word guessed scores a point. With the house rule "Skipping costs" each skipped word takes a point off, but a turn never goes below 0.

Taking turns: if you like, play explaining, drawing and acting in turn; the app says which for every turn.

End: once a team reaches the target, the other teams finish the round. Then the team with the most points wins.

Goal

Be the first team to reach the target, 30 guessed words by default.

Scoring at a glance

Teams take turns, within a team the next person explains. One point per word guessed, the sand timer runs 60 seconds. The first team to 30 wins; the other teams finish the round.

Keeping score in the app

The app says which team is up and who explains, runs the sand timer and counts the words guessed. The words come from your own cards or slips.
