Belote

Four players, trumps and belote-rebelote · 4 players · Card games

What is it?

France's most played card game: four players in two teams with 32 cards; one team takes the trump and must score more than the other.

How it’s played

Cards: 32 cards from 7 to ace. In trumps the jack counts 20, the 9 14, the ace 11, the 10 10, the king 4, the queen 3. In the other suits ace 11, 10 10, king 4, queen 3, jack 2. The last trick brings 10 (“dix de der”); 162 in all.

Taking: After the deal one card lies face up. Whoever takes it as trump plays for their team; in a second round another suit may be chosen.

Contract: The team that took must score more than the other, otherwise it is “dedans” and the opponents score 162. A tie is a “litige”.

Belote-rebelote: King and queen of trumps in one hand bring 20. Capot: Taking all eight tricks scores 252.

Goal

Be the first team to reach the target, usually 1000 points.

Scoring at a glance

162 card points per deal, belote 20, capot 252. The taking team must score strictly more, otherwise the opponents score 162. On a tie the taker's points wait for the next deal.

Keeping score in the app

For each deal you enter which team took, the card points of one team, capot and belote, and announcements if you play them. The app decides whether the contract was made or failed, holds the points of a tie for the next deal and ends the game at the target.
