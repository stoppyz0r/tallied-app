Darts Shanghai

One number per round, Shanghai wins outright · 1–8 players · Darts

What is it?

A darts game played over usually seven rounds in which everyone aims at the same number: first the 1, then the 2 and so on. Singles, doubles and triples score single, double and triple. Whoever hits all three in one visit has a Shanghai and wins at once.

How it’s played

Setup: You need a dartboard, three darts each and a throwing line. Agree on how many rounds to play: 7, 10 or 20. Everyone starts with 0 points.

Round target: In round 1 only the 1 counts, in round 2 only the 2 and so on. Taking turns, each player throws a visit of three darts at the round’s number; then the next round begins.

Points: A single scores the round’s number, a double twice that, a triple three times. Other numbers, the bull and misses score nothing. In round 4, a single scores 4, a double 8 and a triple 12 points.

Shanghai: If someone hits a single, a double and a triple of the round’s number in one visit, in any order, that is a Shanghai, and they win at once.

End of the game: Without a Shanghai, the game ends after the last round, and the highest total wins; on a tie, everyone with the highest total shares the win.

Variants: If you like, a bull round follows at the end, where the outer bull scores 25 and the bullseye 50. You can also play without the instant win, in which case a Shanghai just scores its points.

Goal

Win at once with a Shanghai, or have the most points after the last round.

Scoring at a glance

In round n only the number n counts: single once, double twice, triple three times. A single, double and triple of that number in one visit make a Shanghai and win at once. Otherwise the highest total after the last round wins.

Keeping score in the app

Darts game in which only the 1 counts in round 1, only the 2 in round 2 and so on. A single, double and triple of the same number in one visit make a Shanghai and win at once. You tap each dart, and the app counts the points and spots the Shanghai.
