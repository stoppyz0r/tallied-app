Darts Killer

Hit your double, then take lives · 2–8 players · Darts

What is it?

A darts elimination game in which everyone defends their own number between 1 and 20. Only after hitting the double of your own number may you attack as a killer and take lives from others by hitting their doubles. The last player with lives left wins.

How it’s played

Setup: You need a dartboard, three darts each and a throwing line. Everyone gets their own number from 1 to 20, with no repeats – for example by each player throwing one dart with their weaker hand. Unless you choose, the app assigns 20, 19, 18 and so on. Everyone starts with the same number of lives, usually three.

Becoming a killer: Players take turns throwing a visit of up to three darts. First you must hit the double of your own number, the narrow outer ring. That makes you a killer from the next dart on and for the rest of the game.

Attacking: As a killer, every hit on another player’s double takes one of their lives. Singles, triples, the bull and numbers nobody has do nothing; anyone who isn’t a killer yet can’t take lives. If a killer hits their own double, they lose a life themselves.

Elimination: Anyone with no lives left is out and stops throwing; their remaining darts are dropped.

End of the game: Once only one player has lives left, they win at once.

Variants: The number of lives (one to nine) and whether a killer loses a life for hitting their own double can be set when you create the game.

Goal

Become a killer, take everyone else’s lives and keep your own. The last player left wins.

Scoring at a glance

Everyone has their own number and the same number of lives. Your own double makes you a killer. As a killer, each hit on another player’s double costs them a life, and a hit on your own double costs you one. With no lives you are out; the last player with lives wins.

Keeping score in the app

Darts game in which everyone has their own number. Whoever hits their own double becomes a killer and takes lives from others by hitting their doubles. Anyone with no lives left is out. You tap each dart, and the app keeps track of lives, killers and turn order.
