Darts Count-Up

Eight visits, everything counts · 1–8 players · Darts

What is it?

A darts game without special rules: you throw a set number of visits and add up all the points.

How it’s played

Play: In turn each player throws three darts. Every dart counts its value, doubles and triples twice or three times, the bull 25 or 50.

End: After eight visits (or the agreed number) the highest total wins. The most is 180 per visit.

Goal

Have the most points after all visits.

Scoring at a glance

Every visit counts its points; after eight visits the highest total wins.

Keeping score in the app

The simplest darts game: every visit counts its points. Good as a warm-up, alone or against each other. You tap each dart, and the app adds up and remembers your best.
