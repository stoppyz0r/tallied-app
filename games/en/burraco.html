Burraco

Two or four players, burracos and pozzetto · 2, 4 players · Card games

What is it?

Italy's favourite card game, a relative of canasta: two or four players with two decks and four jokers. Laying runs and sets of seven cards makes a burraco.

How it’s played

Cards: joker 30, pinella (the two, also wild) 20, ace 15, king to 8 each 10, 7 to 3 each 5.

Playing: On your turn you draw a card or take the whole discard pile, meld runs of one suit and sets of equal cards and discard a card. Whoever runs out of cards takes the pozzetto, eleven cards set aside at the start, and plays on.

Burraco: Seven or more cards in a run or a set. Clean without wild cards 200, semi-clean 150, dirty with a wild card 100.

Closing: A side that took its pozzetto and has a burraco can close by getting rid of all cards and gets 100. Cards left in hand count minus, a pozzetto not taken −100.

Goal

Be the first side to reach the target, usually 2005 points.

Scoring at a glance

Clean burraco 200, semi-clean 150, dirty 100, closing 100, plus the cards on the table; cards in hand count minus, a pozzetto not taken −100. First to 2005 wins.

Keeping score in the app

For each hand you enter who closed and, per side, the burracos by kind, the card points on the table and in hand and whether it took its pozzetto. The app scores bonuses and deductions and ends the game at the target.
