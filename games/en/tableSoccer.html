Table Soccer

To 10 goals, usually best of three · 2–4 players · Sports

What is it?

Soccer on a table: players are fixed on rotating rods, and each side uses them to shoot the ball into the opposing goal. Played by two or four.

How it’s played

Lineup: With two, each player handles all four rods of their side; with four, a team splits defense (goalkeeper and defenders) and attack (midfield and forwards).

Kick-off: The ball is dropped in the middle or served from the midfield rod. After a goal, the side that conceded kicks off.

Playing: Rods are only pushed and turned, never spun all the way round. If the ball lies still out of reach, it is dropped in the middle again. A goal counts when the ball stays in the goal.

End: A game usually goes to 10 goals, some play to 6 or 7. Play to two games won; with four, teams often switch ends or positions between games.

Goal

Score the agreed number of goals first and so win the most games.

Scoring at a glance

A game goes to 10 goals, no lead needed. After a goal the side that conceded kicks off. Usually best of three.

Keeping score in the app

One against one or two against two at the foosball table. After each game you enter the goals – the app checks the score, counts the games won and keeps the ratings.
