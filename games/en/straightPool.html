Straight Pool

Every ball a point, up to the target · 2 players · Sports

What is it?

The pool game where every ball potted scores a point and 14 balls are racked again and again. You need a pool table, 15 balls, the cue ball and two cues.

How it’s played

Break: The 15 balls are racked in a triangle. The breaker must call and pot a ball or drive two balls and the cue ball to a cushion; otherwise it costs 2 points and the opponent decides who plays on or whether to break again.

Inning: Every called ball potted scores 1 point, whatever its number. Whoever pots stays at the table. When only one ball is left, the other 14 are racked again and play goes on without a break.

Fouls: A foul costs 1 point. Three fouls in a row cost another 15 points. Scores may go below zero.

Target: The first to reach the agreed score wins.

Goal

Be the first to reach the agreed points, usually 50 or 100.

Scoring at a glance

Every ball potted scores 1 point. Foul −1, foul on the break −2, three fouls in a row another −15. Whoever reaches the target wins.

Keeping score in the app

After every inning you enter the balls potted and whether it ended with a foul. The app scores the points, fouls, the foul on the break and three fouls in a row, shows the balls on the table and says when to rack again.
