Darts Cricket

Close 20 to 15 and bull, then score · 1–8 players · Darts

What is it?

A tactical darts game with seven targets: the numbers 15 to 20 and the bull. You close each target with three hits, with doubles and triples counting two and three. Whoever closes a target early can score on it while the others still have it open.

How it’s played

Setup: You need a dartboard, three darts each and a throwing line. The targets are 20, 19, 18, 17, 16, 15 and the bull; everyone starts with no hits and no points. Players take turns, each throwing a visit of up to three darts.

Counting hits: A single on a target counts one hit, a double two, a triple three. The outer bull counts one hit, the bullseye two. Numbers 1 to 14 and misses don’t count. With three hits a target is closed for you. You may close the targets in any order.

Scoring: Every further hit on a target you have closed scores as many points as the number, 25 for the bull – but only while at least one other player still has that target open. One dart can close and score at once: if you have two hits on 20 and throw a triple 20, one hit closes it and the other two score 40 points. Once everyone has closed a target, it scores nothing more.

End of the game: Whoever has closed all seven targets and has at least as many points as everyone else wins immediately; the remaining darts of the visit are dropped. If you are still behind on points, keep scoring on targets others still have open. Playing alone, the practice ends once all targets are closed.

Variants: In cut-throat, the players who still have the target open get the points, and in the end the lowest score wins. Without points, whoever closes everything first simply wins. If you like, the game ends after a fixed number of rounds.

Goal

Close all seven targets while at least level on points. The first to do so wins.

Scoring at a glance

A single counts one hit, a double two, a triple three; outer bull one, bullseye two. Three hits close a target (20 to 15, bull). Further hits score the number, 25 for the bull, as long as someone else still has the target open. Whoever has closed everything and isn’t behind on points wins at once.

Keeping score in the app

Darts game in which everyone closes the 20 to 15 and the bull with three hits each. Once you have closed a number, you score on it as long as someone else still has it open. You tap each dart, and the app counts hits and points and spots the winner.
