Chinchón

Rummy with knock-outs and rejoins · 2–8 players · Card games

What is it?

Spain’s rummy with 40 or 48 Spanish cards: you collect sets and runs, close once almost everything fits, and whoever goes over 100 points is out. Seven cards of one suit in a row are a chinchón and win at once.

How it’s played

Cards: 40 or 48 Spanish cards (two packs from five players). Unmatched cards count their value, jack, horse and king 10 each (or 10, 11, 12).

Playing: Everyone gets seven cards. On your turn you draw from the stock or the discard pile and discard one card. Combinations are three or four of a kind or at least three cards of one suit in a row. With only a few unmatched points left (usually 3 or 5) you close: everybody shows their combinations, the others may lay off.

Points: Everyone writes down the points of their unmatched cards. Closing with all cards matched counts −10. A chinchón (seven cards of one suit in a row) wins the match at once. Going over 100 points puts you out, but often you may rejoin for a stake with the points of the highest player (“reengancharse”). The last one left wins.

Goal

Be the last one under 100 points – or lay a chinchón.

Scoring at a glance

Unmatched cards count their value, face cards 10. Closing with everything matched counts −10; a chinchón wins at once. Over 100 you are out; once you may rejoin with the points of the highest player. The last one left wins.

Keeping score in the app

For each hand you enter who closed and the points of the unmatched cards – with the card calculator or as a number. The app gives −10 for everything matched, puts out whoever passes 100, offers the rejoin with the points of the highest player and knows the winner at once on a chinchón.
