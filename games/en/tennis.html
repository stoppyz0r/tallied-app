Tennis

Point by point: 15, 30, 40, game · 2, 4 players · Sports

What is it?

The racket game on a court with a net, rackets and a felt ball, played as singles or doubles.

How it’s played

Serve: The server stands behind the baseline and serves diagonally into the service box opposite, alternating from the right and the left. You have two tries per point. The serve changes after every game; in doubles all four serve in turn.

Points: A point goes to whoever last played the ball into the court correctly – when the other side fails to return it after at most one bounce, or hits it out or into the net.

Game: The count goes 15, 30, 40, then game. At 40:40 it’s deuce: whoever then wins two points in a row wins the game (first advantage, then game). If you like, the next point at deuce decides.

Set: The first to six games with two games ahead wins the set (6:4, 7:5). At 6:6 a tiebreak follows, to 7 points with two ahead; the serve changes after the first point, then every two points.

Change of ends: After the first, third, fifth … game of each set, and every six points in the tiebreak, the players change ends.

Match: Usually to two sets; instead of a third set a match tiebreak to 10 is often played.

Goal

Be the first to win the agreed number of sets; a set goes to six games with two games ahead.

Scoring at a glance

15, 30, 40, game; at 40:40 deuce, then two points in a row. A set goes to 6 games with two ahead, at 6:6 a tiebreak to 7. Usually two sets to win.

Keeping score in the app

Singles or doubles on the court. After each point you tap who won it – the app counts 15, 30, 40, deuce, games, sets and the tiebreak, says who serves and when to change ends, and shows break, set and match points. Whole sets can be entered afterwards, too.
