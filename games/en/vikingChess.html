Viking Chess

Knock down the blocks, then the king · 2–12 players · Ball, throwing and lane games

What is it?

A throwing game from Scandinavia with wooden blocks and a king in the middle of the field. Two teams throw sticks at the other side's blocks; whoever has knocked them all down may throw at the king.

How it’s played

Setup: The field is about 5 by 8 meters. Five base blocks stand on each baseline, the king in the middle. Each team has six sticks.

Throwing: The starting team throws the sticks underhand, spinning end over end, from its baseline at the other side's base blocks. The other team then throws the knocked-down blocks into the throwing team's half, where they are stood up as field blocks.

Field blocks: The team whose turn it is must first knock down all field blocks in the opposing half before base blocks count. If a field block stays up, the other team may throw from that block's line on its next turn.

The king: Once a team has knocked down all of the other side's field and base blocks, it may throw its remaining sticks at the king from the baseline. If he falls, it wins the set. Whoever knocks the king down earlier loses the set at once.

End: Usually played to two sets; in tournaments the standing base blocks often count too.

Goal

Knock down all of the other side's blocks, then the king – and so win the agreed number of sets.

Scoring at a glance

First the field blocks, then the other side's base blocks, then the king. If the king falls too early, the set is lost. Usually to two sets.

Keeping score in the app

Lawn throwing game for two teams. After each set you tap which team won, with the standing base blocks if you like – the app counts the sets to the win and keeps the teams.
