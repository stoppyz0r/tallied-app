Watten

Name rank and trumps, raise, bluff · 2, 4 players · Card games

What is it?

A trick-taking and bluffing game from Bavaria and Tyrol played with German-suited cards. Everyone holds just five cards, rank and trumps change every round, and three fixed top cards – king of hearts, 7 of bells and 7 of acorns – beat everything. Raising increases the round’s value; whoever won’t go along gives the round up.

How it’s played

Setup: This describes the Bavarian way with 32 cards: 7, 8, 9, 10, Unter, Ober, king and ace in acorns, leaves, hearts and bells. With four players, partners sit opposite each other; with two, each plays alone. Everyone gets five cards, and the rest stay aside. Dealing and play go clockwise; the player to the dealer’s left is forehand.

Rank and trumps: At first only forehand and the dealer look at their cards. Forehand names a rank, such as Ober; then the dealer names the trump suit. Only then do the others pick up their cards.

Ranking: At the very top are always the three critical cards: king of hearts (Max), 7 of bells (Belli) and 7 of acorns (Spitz). Next comes the main card, the card of the named rank in the trump suit, then the other cards of that rank; among equal ranks the one played first wins. Then come the remaining trumps and finally the other suits, each ace, king, Ober, Unter, 10, 9, 8, 7.

Tricks: Forehand leads, and everyone else plays one card in turn. You don’t have to follow suit. Only if the main card is led in the first trick with the call “trump or critical” must you play a trump or a critical card if you have one. The highest critical card wins the trick, otherwise the highest card of the named rank, otherwise the highest trump, otherwise the highest card of the suit led. The winner of a trick leads the next. The first side to take three tricks wins the round; card points don’t count.

Raising: A round starts out worth 2 points. While it is running, a side may raise it by one point. If the other side goes along, play continues for the higher value. If not, the round ends at once, and the raising side gets the value before the raise. Raises alternate between the sides.

End of the game: After each round the next player deals. The first side to reach the target – 13, 15 or 18 points, usually 15 – wins the game.

Goal

Win points by taking three tricks or with a raise the other side won’t go along with. The first to reach the target, usually 15, wins.

Scoring at a glance

With four players, partners sit opposite each other. Three tricks win the round. It is worth 2 points, and each accepted raise adds 1. If a side won’t go along, the raising side gets the value before the raise. The first to reach the target (13, 15 or 18) wins.

Keeping score in the app

Trick-taking and bluffing game from Bavaria and Tyrol, played by four in two teams or by two. Rank and trumps are chosen anew each round, three tricks win the round, and raising increases its value. You tap who won the round and what it was worth, and the app keeps the score up to the target.
